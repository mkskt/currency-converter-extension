Target Currencies

How to Use

Simply highlight any price text on a webpage. A tooltip box will overlay tracking rates converted to your target currencies.

Exchange Rates

Rates update automatically once every 24 hours.

Data Privacy

This extension functions entirely client-side. No tracking networks, operational analytics, or logging telemetry layers exist. Personal browsing metrics are never collected.

Instant Conversion

Highlight any price on a website to view conversions directly beside your cursor.

Click to Copy

Click any row inside the hovering tooltip to copy its value.

Activation Keys

Configure an activation key so that conversions only appear when you want them to. After setting it up, simply hold down set key while highlighting.

Blacklist

Don't want to see any conversions on certain websites? Simply add them to the blacklist.
